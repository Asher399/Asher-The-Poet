My Story

More than a writer.

My name is Jacob Stephen Mphampha, and I write under the name Asher The Poet.

Writing, to me, is more than putting words on a page. It is a way of preserving thoughts, challenging ordinary thinking, encouraging people and giving a voice to ideas that deserve to be heard.

My journey is driven by curiosity, faith, ambition and a desire to become better while helping others see possibilities beyond their present circumstances.

Through poetry, books, inspirational writing and creative expression, I seek to create work that does not simply entertain the reader — but leaves the reader thinking.

"A writer does not merely write words. A writer leaves pieces of thought in the minds of others."

Welcome to my world of words.
