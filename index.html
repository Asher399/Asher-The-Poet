The Story

Meet the mind
behind the words.

Jacob Stephen Mphampha, creatively known as Asher The Poet, is a writer driven by curiosity, imagination and a desire to communicate ideas that matter.

For me, writing is not simply about filling pages. A powerful sentence can challenge a belief. A poem can give language to an emotion. A book can introduce someone to an entirely different way of seeing life.

My work explores love, life, purpose, ambition, faith, personal growth and the psychology of people.

I am still on the journey — learning, creating and developing a body of work that I hope will continue to speak to people long after the words have been written.

"I don't write simply because I have words. I write because I have something worth saying."

The Philosophy

Think. Create.
Inspire.

Three principles that guide the work and creative journey of Asher The Poet.

01 — THINK

Challenge Ideas

Growth begins when we become willing to question what we know, learn something new and look beyond the obvious.

02 — CREATE

Give Ideas Form

An idea becomes powerful when imagination is transformed into something that another person can experience.

03 — INSPIRE

Leave Something Behind

The value of our work is measured not only by what we accomplish, but by what we inspire other people to become.
