About Asher The Poet

A Voice Built Through Words.

Jacob Stephen Mphampha

Known professionally as Asher The Poet

Poet • Author • Storyteller

Asher The Poet, whose real name is Jacob Stephen Mphampha, is a Malawian poet, writer, storyteller and emerging author with a passion for literature and meaningful expression.

His writing explores subjects such as love, relationships, psychology, emotions, personal development, wisdom, purpose and human behavior.

Through poetry and literary works, he seeks to transform thoughts and experiences into words that encourage reflection, inspire growth and create meaningful conversations.

His growing body of literary work includes The Broken Silence, The Psychology of Love, Rise Above Average and The Wisdom of Love.

Asher believes that words carry responsibility. A writer can entertain, educate, challenge, encourage and inspire. His ambition is therefore not simply to write, but to create work that leaves something valuable in the mind of the reader.

His journey as a young Malawian writer continues to develop, with a long-term vision of creating meaningful literature that reaches readers beyond borders.

— Asher The Poet
FAITH & PURPOSE

A Servant of God

✝

Faith. Purpose. Service.

Beyond writing and creativity, Asher The Poet identifies himself as a Servant of God. His faith forms an important part of his values, purpose and personal journey.

He seeks to use the gifts entrusted to him responsibly — using words to encourage, inspire, teach, reflect and communicate messages of hope, wisdom, love, faith and purpose.

“Let my words carry meaning, my life carry purpose, and my gifts serve God.”

Why I Write

Every word is an opportunity to make a difference.
