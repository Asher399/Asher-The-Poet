The Author

More than words.

Jacob Stephen Mphampha, known as Asher The Poet, is an emerging author and poet passionate about literature, human psychology, relationships, personal development, wisdom and purpose.

Through writing, he seeks to turn thoughts into words, experiences into lessons and ideas into messages that can influence lives.

His work is built around one simple belief: words have the power to change the way people think, live and see the world.

— Asher The Poet
