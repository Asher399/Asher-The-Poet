01 — The Author

Words with
meaning.

Poetry is more than words. It is emotion, thought, experience and truth transformed into something that can live beyond the moment.

01

Jacob Stephen Mphampha , professionally known as Asher The Poet , is a poet, author and storyteller whose work is inspired by the realities of life, relationships, human behaviour, personal growth and faith.

Through poetry and books, Asher seeks to transform thoughts into words and words into ideas that challenge, encourage and inspire people to see life differently.

His creative journey is built around one simple belief: the right words can change the way a person thinks.

— Asher The Poet
